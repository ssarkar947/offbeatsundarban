Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Offbeat Sundarban Tour collects, uses, handles, and protects your personal information when you visit our website, submit booking requests, or interact with our advertisements across Meta platforms (Facebook & Instagram Lead Generation Ads).

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 9, 2026 • Applicable Law: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), India

Notice for Users Arriving from Facebook & Instagram Ads

If you submitted your contact details (such as Name, Phone Number, WhatsApp Number, or Group Size) via a Facebook or Instagram Lead Ad form, your data is used solely to contact you regarding your Sundarban tour inquiry, share itineraries, and confirm booking availability. We adhere strictly to Meta's Advertising Policies and will never sell or share your data with unauthorized third parties.

Offbeat Sundarban Tour ("we", "us", or "our") is an eco-tourism and nature expedition operator offering guided 3-day and custom tours across the Sundarban Tiger Reserve in West Bengal, India.

02. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it to us through our website booking form, direct WhatsApp/phone communication, or via Meta / Facebook / Instagram Lead Generation Ads.

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, mobile phone number, WhatsApp number, and optional email address.
  • Trip Preferences: Number of guests (adults/children), preferred tour dates, and food preferences (e.g., Non-Veg with fresh catch, Pure Vegetarian, Jain meals).
  • Government Permit Details: If you proceed with a confirmed tour booking, government-issued photo ID details (Voter ID, Aadhaar, Passport) as mandatory under West Bengal Forest Department rules for entering the Sundarban Tiger Reserve.
  • Technical & Ad Interaction Data: IP address, device type, browser information, and interaction data captured via standard server logs or Meta Pixel to assess ad performance.

03. How We Use Your Information (Purpose)

We use your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes related to planning and executing your tour experience:

  • Inquiry Fulfillment: Contacting you via WhatsApp, telephone, or SMS to provide detailed itinerary documents, cost breakdowns, and available tide windows.
  • Trip Coordination: Reserving hotel rooms, assigning clean safari cruisers, and provisioning fresh, hygienic food tailored to your dietary requirements.
  • Forest Clearances: Submitting official visitor rosters to the West Bengal Forest Department to obtain authorized entry tokens for watchtowers (Burirdabri, Jhingakhali, Sajnekhali, Sudhanyakhali).
  • Customer Support & Safety: Assisting you with transit directions (Canning / Dhamakhali / Godkhali) and emergency support while navigating the delta.

04. Compliance with Meta / Facebook Advertising Policies

We run Lead Generation advertising campaigns on Meta technologies (Facebook and Instagram). In accordance with Meta's Commercial Terms and Lead Ads Terms:

1. We only collect information that is strictly relevant and necessary to assist you with booking a Sundarban tour.

3. By submitting your details on a Facebook or Instagram Lead Ad form, you provide express consent for our team to contact you directly by phone, SMS, or WhatsApp regarding your inquiry.
4. You may revoke this consent at any time (see Section 08).

For information on how Meta collects and processes your personal data before delivering your lead submission to us, please review Meta's Privacy Policy.

05. Zero Spam & Strict No-Sale Guarantee

We Never Sell or Rent Your Data

Offbeat Sundarban Tour will NEVER sell, lease, rent, trade, or share your contact number, name, or travel information with any third-party marketing companies, data brokers, or advertising networks. Your contact details remain strictly between you and our tour coordinating team.

06. Sharing with Authorized Service Partners

We only share your necessary details with authorized entities strictly to carry out your tour:

  • West Bengal Forest Department: As required by statutory Indian wildlife protection laws for entry permits and watchtower passes.
  • Local Hotel & Boat Crew: Guest headcounts and dietary choices (veg/non-veg) shared with the boat cooks and hotel desk to prepare rooms and meals.
  • Law Enforcement / Legal Obligations: If legally compelled by Indian judicial authorities, forest guards, or emergency response services.

07. Data Security & Retention

We maintain appropriate administrative and technical measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Retention Period: Lead information collected through Facebook Ads or website inquiries is retained only as long as necessary to answer your inquiry and manage active bookings. If you do not book a tour, your contact record can be purged upon request or after our seasonal inquiry cycle ends.

08. Your Rights & How to Opt-Out

Under Indian digital data privacy regulations, you have full ownership and rights over your personal data:

  • Right to Opt-Out / Stop Messages: You can stop receiving WhatsApp messages or calls from us at any time by simply replying with "STOP" or letting our coordinator know.
  • Right to Access & Rectify: You can ask us what details we hold for you or request corrections to your name or contact number.
  • Right to Erasure (Deletion): You can request immediate and permanent deletion of your phone number and lead record from our databases.

To exercise any of these rights, message or call us directly at +91 7384593640. We process all opt-out and deletion requests within 24 to 48 business hours.

09. Cookies & Meta Pixel Technologies

Our website may use standard cookies and the Meta Pixel (formerly Facebook Pixel) to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns and understand aggregated visitor interactions. These tools do not capture personally identifiable details (such as your bank data or passwords). You can configure your browser settings to refuse cookies or manage your ad preferences directly inside your Facebook / Instagram account settings.
