# Offbeat Sundarban Tour

Official website for **Offbeat Sundarban Tour** — a quiet, authentic journey through the mangrove delta away from crowded commercial tourism.

---

## 🌿 Overview & Highlights

- **3 Days / 2 Nights Tour (₹3,500 per person)**:
  - **Day 1**: Pickup from Canning &rarr; transfer to Dhamakhali &rarr; board the boat to visit Sundarban Village, Bird Forest, Hemnagar Ship Port, and Zero Point. Overnight stay in a clean riverside hotel.
  - **Day 2**: Deep forest exploration to Burirdabri Watch Tower (with panoramic view of Bangladesh forest across the Raimangal river), Khatua Juri Forest, Morichjhapi Forest, and Harikhali Watch Tower.
  - **Day 3**: Local delta market walk, Jhingakhali Watch Tower, and hot lunch on the boat back to Dhamakhali.
- **Wildlife & Birding Photography Tours**: Custom trips planned around golden light and low tides with low-vibration idling and beanbag camera supports.
- **Fresh Food Cooked on Board**: Morning luchi and alur dom, fragrant Gobindobhog rice, yellow dal, aloo bhaja, fresh bhetki fish jhol, giant river prawns (*golda chingri*) in coconut gravy, and evening chai. 100% sealed mineral water used for all cooking and drinking.
- **Local Guides & Storytellers**: Travel with boatmen and guides who grew up in the delta, know bird calls, track pugmarks on the mudflats, and share local folklore of Ma Bonbibi.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: Direct File Open
Open `index.html` directly in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

### Option 2: Run the Built-in Server
```bash
node start-server.js
```
The server will start at `http://localhost:3000` and automatically open in your default browser.
