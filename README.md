# Offbeat Sundarban Tour

Official website for **Offbeat Sundarban Tour** — a quiet, authentic journey through the mangrove delta away from crowded commercial tourism.

---

## 🌿 Overview & Highlights

- **The Complete Sundarban Tiger Reserve Tour (₹3,999 per person)**:
  - **Day 1: Riverside Villages & Island Exploration**: Pickup from Canning &rarr; transfer to Dhamakhali &rarr; board the boat to visit Sundarban Village, Bird Forest, Hemnagar Ship Port, and Zero Point. Overnight stay in a clean riverside hotel.
  - **Day 2: Deep Forest Exploration**: Early morning departure to Jhingakhali Watch Tower, Burirdabri Watch Tower (with panoramic views of the Bangladesh forest across the Raimangal river), Morichjhapi Forest, Arbesia Forest, Khatuajhuri Forest, and Gapbani Forest.
  - **Day 3: Watch Towers & Forest Safari**: Sajnekhali Watch Tower, Sudhanyakhali Watch Tower, Bonbibi Bharani, and Sarakkhali Forest. Conclude at Godkhali, completing the full cross-delta Tiger Reserve route.
- **Rich Culinary Experience on Board**: Chemical-free vegetables naturally farmed in delta soil, fresh sweetwater fish farmed in Sundarban village ponds (*pukur-er machh*), and wild catch netted from tidal rivers, cooked live in the boat galley and served hot on deck with scenic mangrove views. 100% sealed branded mineral water used for all cooking and drinking.
- **Wildlife & Birding Photography Tours**: Custom trips planned around golden light and low tides with low-vibration idling and beanbag camera supports.
- **Local Biodiversity Guides & Storytellers**: Travel with boatmen and guides who grew up in the delta, know bird calls, track pugmarks on the mudflats, and share local folklore of Ma Bonbibi.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: Direct File Open
Open `index.html` directly in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

### Option 2: Run the Built-in Server
```bash
node start-server.js
```
The server will start at `http://localhost:3000` and automatically open in your default browser.
